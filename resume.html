Frequently asked questions

Who is the LMIS for?

Youth, employers, training providers and the Municipality.

What can youth do on it?

Map their skills, see skills gaps, and find training, internships, apprenticeships and jobs.

How can employers use it?

Post jobs and engage with youth in a structured way. An employer dashboard will track partnerships.

Who will run it long term?

The plan is to anchor it under a Youth Desk or Assembly structure so it lasts beyond the project.
